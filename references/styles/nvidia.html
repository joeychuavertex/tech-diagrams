NVIDIA blueprints style

Match the look of build.nvidia.com/blueprints. Pure-black canvas, white sans-serif text, thin (1px) white-bordered sections with no fill and a centered or top-left uppercase title. Services are rendered as green isometric cubes (#76B900); only non-service primitives (orchestrator, data store, adapter, user) get accent colors. Arrows are 1.25px white with white labels positioned above the midpoint.

Container conventions

Outer canvas — solid black #000 page background.
Sections — 1px white border, no fill, sharp corners (rx 0–2). White uppercase title can sit above the border (no pill cutout) or top-left inside.
Nested sections are allowed; keep stroke uniformly 1px and don't add fills.
Avoid color fills on group containers — color belongs on the icons, not the boxes.

Icon conventions

Default service node = isometric green cube (ico-nv-service) at 50–64px. The cube label carries the service name (e.g. "NeMo Customizer", "NIM", "Triton"). Reserve accent colors for non-service primitives: orchestrator (yellow gear), datastore (orange cylinder), adapter (purple diamond), user/admin (blue circle), doc/report (orange page).

Connector conventions

Single arrow style — 1.25px white solid with a small white arrowhead. Labels sit above the segment midpoint in white 11px regular weight. Avoid dashed/multicolor flows; the NVIDIA blueprint aesthetic is monochromatic on top of colored icons. If you need a secondary path, use a thinner #888 stroke rather than a different color.
