Google Cloud architecture style

Match the look of docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/blueprints/security-foundations and the broader cloud.google.com/architecture center. White page, light-gray solid project outline, dashed gray for regions/zones, solid Google-blue for VPC. Service icons are filled rounded badges using the Google 4-color palette (blue / red / yellow / green) with a single white glyph. Connectors are gray #5F6368 with simple arrowheads; step markers are blue circles with white numbers.

Container conventions

Project — solid #DADCE0 1.5px, "Google Cloud" chip top-left (4-color stripe + label).
Region — dashed #80868B; Zone — dashed #BDC1C6.
VPC — solid Google-blue #4285F4 1.5px.
Subnet — dashed Google-blue (lighter, 1px).

Icon conventions

Rounded square badge (rx=8) filled with one of the four Google brand colors based on category, with a white stroked glyph inside. Title 12px bold below the icon, subtitle 10.5px muted gray. Use the badge color to encode the category and the label to encode the service (e.g. ico-gcp-database + label "Spanner" or "Cloud SQL" or "Firestore").

Connector conventions

Solid #5F6368 1.5px lines for request paths; dashed lighter #BDC1C6 for async / ingestion paths. Step markers are 9px-radius blue circles with white bold numbers (request) or letters (ingestion).
